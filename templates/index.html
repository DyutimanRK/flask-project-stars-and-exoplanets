Welcome to the Universe

The night sky contains billions of stars and an enormous number of planets. Stars are massive objects that produce energy through nuclear fusion, while planets orbit stars and do not produce their own light. Our Solar System is just one planetary system among many in the Universe.

Stars

Stars are huge, extremely hot balls of plasma held together by gravity. They are mainly made of hydrogen and helium. The Sun is the closest star to Earth and provides most of the energy that makes life on our planet possible.

A Universe Full of Worlds

An exoplanet is a planet that orbits a star outside our Solar System. Astronomers have discovered thousands of confirmed exoplanets, ranging from small rocky worlds to enormous gas giants. Some orbit their stars in only a few days, while others take many years to complete one orbit.

Scientists study exoplanets to understand how planetary systems form and evolve. They are also interested in planets that may have conditions suitable for liquid water and potentially life, although being in a habitable zone does not by itself prove that a planet can support life.
